Redis Chroma

Sem-Cache-Router

A gateway between an app and an LLM provider. It answers a repeated question from Redis in about a millisecond, a reworded one from ChromaDB by meaning, and only asks the provider when neither has an answer. Ask something below and watch where the answer came from.

Ask

    Rate limit

    Every caller has a token budget that refills over time. Send a burst of new questions at once and watch the gateway refuse the ones over budget with a 429 and a wait, then let them through again once the budget refills. Everyone on this page shares one budget.

    Failover

    The provider here is the mock vendor API. Break the primary and ask a new question: the circuit breaker sends it to the secondary and the caller never sees an error.

    Admin: empty the cache

    Answers come from whichever provider the gateway is configured with.
